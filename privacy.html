Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

At Vikways, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as when you create an account, book a ride, or contact support. This may include:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Payment information (processed securely)
  • Ride history and location data
  • Device information and app usage

2. How we use your information

We use your data to provide, improve, and personalize our services, including:

  • Connecting you with drivers and processing rides
  • Processing payments and preventing fraud
  • Communicating with you about rides and updates
  • Improving safety and customer support

3. Sharing your information

We share your information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With drivers to complete your ride
  • With trusted third-party service providers (payment processing, analytics)
  • When required by law or to protect safety
  • With your explicit consent

4. Data security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption, secure servers, and regular security audits. However, no system is completely secure, and we encourage you to protect your account credentials.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@vikways.com.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. We'll notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification. Please review this policy regularly.

For any privacy-related questions, please contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@vikways.com.
